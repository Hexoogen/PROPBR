## Default synthetic demonstration

Settings: seed 42, two synthetic leaves, 400–2500 nm at 10 nm intervals, float64, 1,200 AdamW steps and three starting points. The synthetic parameters are generated independently of the optimizer's starting points.

| Model and geometry | Maximum spectral RMSE across the two synthetic leaves |
|---|---|
| PROPBR, fixed 35° | 7.39 × 10⁻⁵ |
| PROPBR, joint 15°/35°/55° | 2.86 × 10⁻¹⁷ |
| sPROPBR, no geometric input | 6.03 × 10⁻¹⁷ |

Near-machine-precision fits are possible for noiseless spectra generated by the same model. They are an execution and recovery check, not evidence of accuracy on real leaves or of unique parameter identifiability.

EFAST used N=1,000 at 50 nm intervals: 9,000 forward evaluations for PROPBR including phase angle, and 8,000 for sPROPBR. N is the sample count per input factor, as defined by SALib's FAST sampler.

The complete plotting demo was verified with the CPU environment. An older local CUDA environment had conflicting OpenMP libraries during plotting; no workaround that suppresses OpenMP safety checks is included. CUDA model, derivative and inversion agreement was verified independently.

## Relationship to the historical experiments

The implementation follows the manuscript's equations and corrects repeated fsurf multiplication, restores angular Schlick reflectance and applies the roughness-dependent reference subtraction. Measured-data scores and runtime comparisons from historical scripts need to be evaluated again with this implementation and external datasets.
